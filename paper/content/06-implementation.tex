\section{Application-Specific Integrated Circuit ({ASIC}) Implementation}
\label{sec:impl}

% Locked receipts only. No 100 MHz overlay / Dhrystone / old GDS
% without vector. No 3 GHz target / WNS in the PDF.

I ran the {SoC}---scalar core, {Zve32x}, and the {GEMM}---through
Open{ROAD}~\cite{ajayi2019openroad} on the {ASAP7} 7\,nm predictive
Process Design Kit ({PDK})~\cite{clark2016asap7}.
Memories are ports, so Table~\ref{tab:asap7} is logic only.
Figure~\ref{fig:die} is that finish.
Post-route $F_{\max}$ is 540.23\,{MHz}.
